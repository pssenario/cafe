## Brand & Style

This design system establishes the visual and structural language for the **Centro Industrial y de Energías Alternativas (CIEA) - Regional La Guajira** under the institutional authority of SENA Colombia. It serves technical graduates, industrial stakeholders, regional energy consortia, and prospective apprentices looking for employment observatories and vocational training across solar, wind, sustainable mining, and regional agro-industrial sectors.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The design blends **Corporate/Institutional Modernism** with an **Industrial-Clean Tech** clarity. It moves away from bureaucratic density toward an open, high-legibility interface that balances governmental trustworthiness with technical forwardness. The aesthetic features crisp typography, generous whitespace, structured data modules, and clear visual signifiers of sustainable power (sun, wind, desert, and Caribbean maritime currents) anchored firmly by the recognized SENA institutional green.

### Emotional Goals & Audience Touchpoints
- **Authority & Trust:** Adheres strictly to modern Colombian public administration standards, compliant with accessible public web portals (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- **Technological Progress:** Expresses the regional transition toward clean energy (solar farms in Maicao and Uribia, offshore/onshore wind turbines) via technical indicators, data cards, and clean infographics.
- **Empowerment & Employment:** Delivers immediate, low-friction scanning of technical certification profiles, job boards, labor observatory metrics, and regional impact indicators.

## Colors

The palette balances SENA's constitutional green with regional, technical energy signatures specific to La Guajira.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#007A33` - Deep Institutional Green):** Selected as the primary interactive and stabilizing brand hue. While the extracted `#39A900` represents the vibrant institutional mark, `#007A33` fulfills strict WCAG AA contrast guidelines for body text, primary action buttons, key metrics, and navigational anchors when paired with white backgrounds.
- **Brand Accent Green (`#39A900`):** Used selectively for highlights, status indicators ("Active", "Available Candidate"), high-visibility badges, progress bars, and thematic accents.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Energy Sky / Maritime Blue):** Represents wind and offshore power generation, hydrogen energy transition, and technical engineering certifications. Used for technical category tags, secondary actions, and observatory chart axes.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Guajira Solar Amber):** Evokes the desert sun, solar photovoltaic programs, high-priority notifications, and regional cultural vibrancy. Applied intentionally in callout chips, stats highlights, and warning banners.
- **Institutional Navy (`#0B3553`):** Anchors headers, prominent section titles, and institutional footers, giving governmental weight without visual harshness.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` - Slate Charcoal):** Governs standard body typography, complex table data, form labels, and neutral icon outlines.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:** `#FFFFFF` serves as the primary card and container base, sitting on `#F8FAFC` to isolate modules cleanly without heavy borders.

## Typography

The design system exclusively uses **Work Sans**, directly aligning with SENA's national brand identity. Work Sans provides geometric precision, wide aperture letterforms, and high legibility across dense administrative tables, technical profiles, and low-connectivity mobile environments.

### Rules of Hierarchy
- **Strict Scale Progression:** Headlines use bold (`700`) and semi-bold (`600`) weights with negative tracking to build firm industrial authority without visual noise.
- **Body Comfort:** Body text runs exclusively between `14px` and `18px` with generous 1.5x line heights, meeting ergonomic reading standards for complex technical curriculums and job post descriptions.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Badges, energy statistics (e.g., kW/h capacity, placement rates), and form captions leverage `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`0.025em`) in medium or semi-bold weights to retain legibility even at smaller scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt grid system configured inside a fluid 12-column responsive layout, capped at a maximum desktop container width of `1280px` to maintain balanced line-lengths.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Mobile (320px – 639px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. All dual-column action groups collapse to full-width vertically stacked triggers.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Dashboard summary widgets and labor metrics reflow into a 2x2 grid.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Full asymmetric layout accommodates permanent side filters for job catalogs, certification registers, and telemetry summaries.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Internal Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`):** Reserved for button padding, card interiors, and field enclosures.
- **Section Spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`):** Separates discrete functional clusters such as regional metrics from alumni testimonial feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, skepticism-inducing skeumorphism, or high-blur glassmorphic treatments. In a technical public utility ecosystem, clarity and immediate comprehension are essential. Depth is achieved primarily through **tonal layering** and **refined micro-borders**.

### Surface Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F8FAFC` creates a subtle neutral foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. This flat-structure approach retains maximum visual contrast under daylight screen conditions.
- **Layer 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Modals):** Uses a crisp, low-opacity ambient drop shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 53, 83, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 53, 83, 0.04)`. The shadow is tinted with Institutional Navy (`#0B3553`) rather than muddy black, reflecting technical fidelity.
- **Interactive State Elevation:** Hovering over actionable cards (e.g., job postings, graduate profiles) produces a subtle upward translate of `-2px` accompanied by a border color shift to `#0284C7` (Energy Sky) or `#007A33` (SENA Green), without creating distracting visual weight.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft (`1`)** corner geometry. 

- Standard interactive controls, form inputs, badges, and table containers feature a corner radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- Larger layout surfaces, feature hero panels, and prominent modal windows use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status tags and metric pills leverage full-pill structures (`9999px`) to immediately identify non-interactive categorical data.

This shape approach conveys industrial order, functional precision, and engineering discipline, avoiding the playful consumer look of fully-rounded bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in SENA Deep Green (`#007A33`), text in pure `#FFFFFF`, semi-bold `label-md`, radius `4px`, padding `10px 20px`. Hover transitions to `#174300`. Active state contracts with an inward shadow outline.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface in transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in `#007A33`, text `#007A33`. Hover applies background tint `#F0FDF4`.
- **Tertiary / Action Blue Button:** Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, used strictly for technical actions (e.g., "Descargar Ficha Técnica", "Postular Vacante Eólica").
- **Disabled State:** Solid `#E2E8F0` background with `#94A3B8` typography, cursor not-allowed, zero shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Energy Badges:**
  - *Solar Photovoltaic:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`.
  - *Wind / Eólica:* Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#075985`, border `#BAE6FD`.
  - *Sustainable Industry / Mining:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Status Badges:** Small-scale `label-sm` with a 6px leading circular indicator dot (e.g., `#39A900` for active vacancies).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Select Inputs:** Height `44px`, base `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1F2937`, padding `0 12px`. Focus state triggers an immediate 2px outer outline in `#007A33` with zero offset. Helper text utilizes `body-sm` in `#64748B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px x 18px`, border `2px solid #64748B`, corner radius `3px` (checkbox) or circular (radio). Selected state fills with `#007A33` displaying a crisp white SVG check.

### Cards (Graduate Profiles & Vacancies)
- Clean card architecture on white `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top header carries candidate/specialization category chips alongside regional status.
- Middle body displays graduate name, SENA technical credentials, certified hours in renewable energy labs, and core competencies.
- Footer features a divided row containing contract modality, location tag (e.g., "Riohacha", "Uribia", "Fonseca"), and a primary link CTA.

### Data Metric Displays (Labor Observatory)
- Modular data blocks featuring a top accent strip (3px height) in `#F59E0B` (Solar) or `#0284C7` (Wind).
- Prominent numerical value set in `headline-lg` with `Institutional Navy` (`#0B3553`).
- Descriptive label underneath in `label-md` with comparative indicator pill (e.g., "+18% Inserción Laboral").
